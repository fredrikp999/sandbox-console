# How to work

- Before a change that touches more than a couple of files, or that is hard to
  undo, say briefly what you plan to do and why, then go ahead unless the plan
  depends on a choice only the person can make.
- Keep changes focused on what was asked. Mention other problems you notice
  instead of fixing them on the side.
- Match the style, naming and structure of the code around the change.
- When you finish, say what you changed, how you checked it, and anything that
  is left or uncertain.
